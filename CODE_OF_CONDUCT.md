# Community conduct

Keep discussions respectful, specific and focused on the work. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique code and claims with reproducible evidence; do not attack people.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, spam, and disclosure of another person's private data are not acceptable. Do not post session transcripts, credentials or private paths in issues or pull requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation to protect contributors and private data. If a concern needs a private channel, ask a maintainer to establish one without posting sensitive details. See [security reporting](SECURITY.md).
